## Choosing between FCL and LCL shipping from China

FCL and LCL are two different ways to move ocean cargo. The right choice depends on the packed volume, package dimensions, cargo value, handling sensitivity, supplier locations, destination charges and required schedule.

TengYoda reviews these factors before recommending a shipping method. A simple volume threshold is useful for initial planning, but it does not replace an assessment of the actual cargo.

## What is FCL?

FCL means Full Container Load. One shipper or importer uses the container for the booked cargo. Common dry-container types include 20GP, 40GP and 40HQ.

FCL may be suitable when:

- The order is large enough to use a substantial part of a container
- Packages are difficult, fragile or costly to handle repeatedly
- Several supplier orders can be consolidated before loading
- The importer wants a sealed container movement after origin loading
- Destination delivery can receive and unload a container

Theoretical container volume is not the same as usable loading capacity. Package dimensions, door dimensions, weight distribution, pallets, void space and loading equipment all affect the result.

## What is LCL?

LCL means Less than Container Load. Cargo from different shippers is consolidated into one container and separated at the destination warehouse.

LCL may be suitable when:

- The cargo volume is too small for a full container
- The importer is testing a supplier or product
- Smaller orders need to move more frequently
- Goods from several suppliers can be consolidated
- The importer does not have space to receive a full container

LCL involves more cargo handling than FCL. Packaging should be suitable for consolidation, stacking and warehouse movement.

## How LCL charging works

LCL freight is commonly calculated using revenue tonnes or chargeable volume, but the exact rule and minimum charge vary by service. Dense cargo may be charged according to weight, while lighter cargo is usually charged according to cubic metres.

Importers should ask about:

- China receiving and consolidation fees
- Documentation and export charges
- Main ocean freight
- Destination deconsolidation and warehouse charges
- Customs clearance and delivery
- Storage rules if cargo is not collected promptly

## How FCL charging works

FCL quotations normally identify the container type and ocean freight, together with relevant origin and carrier charges. The complete shipment may also involve pickup, loading, customs declaration, documentation, destination charges, truck delivery and empty-container return.

Check whether the quotation is port-to-port, port-to-door or door-to-door. Also confirm the free-time assumptions and responsibility for demurrage or detention.

## Information needed for either method

- Product description, material and intended use
- Package count and packaging type
- Dimensions and gross weight per package
- Total cargo volume and gross weight
- Supplier pickup city
- Destination port or postcode
- Cargo-ready date
- Cargo value and insurance requirement
- Stackability, fragility and handling restrictions
- Dangerous-goods or restricted-product information

## A practical comparison

### Handling

FCL usually involves fewer consolidation and deconsolidation handoffs. LCL cargo shares warehouse and container space with other shipments.

### Cost

LCL can reduce the initial freight cost for smaller volumes. As volume increases, FCL may become more economical, but the break-even point depends on the route and destination charges.

### Schedule

Both services depend on carrier schedules. LCL also requires consolidation and destination deconsolidation time.

### Delivery

FCL requires a plan for container pickup, unloading and empty return. LCL is normally collected from or delivered after the destination warehouse releases the cargo.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is LCL always cheaper than FCL?

No. LCL can be economical for smaller cargo, but origin and destination handling charges increase with volume. Compare the full shipment cost.

### Can several suppliers fill one FCL container?

Yes. Their goods can be consolidated before loading, provided the cargo is compatible and every package is properly identified.

### Which option is safer for fragile cargo?

FCL can reduce handling points, but safe transport still depends on suitable packing, loading and securing. Small fragile shipments can also use LCL when professionally packed.
