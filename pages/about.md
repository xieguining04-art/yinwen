## A China-side logistics team built around importer needs

TengYoda Supply Chain Co., Ltd. is a China-based international logistics business. For more than ten years, our work has centred on coordinating ocean freight, air freight, road transport, warehousing and related freight-forwarding services for importers buying from China.

Our team of more than 100 service professionals develops practical logistics plans around the actual cargo, supplier locations, delivery destination and required schedule. We do not treat the ocean freight rate as the complete shipment plan. Origin pickup, packing, consolidation, export documentation, destination requirements and delivery conditions all need to be considered before booking.

## Operating qualification and carrier coordination

TengYoda holds an NVOCC operating qualification. We maintain booking channels with major carriers and coordinate enquiries involving FCL, LCL and suitable special-equipment shipments.

Carrier selection depends on the cargo, port pair, schedule, equipment availability, transshipment arrangement and commercial requirements. Our regular carrier relationships include services operated by CMA CGM, IAL, Maersk, MCC, COSCO, TSL and KMTC, subject to route and booking availability.

> Qualification documents and company registration details can be supplied during commercial due diligence. Specific certificate numbers should be verified from the current official document before publication.

## Two self-operated consolidation warehouses in Foshan

We operate two professional logistics warehouses in Lishui and Lecong, Foshan. Each facility covers more than 3,000 square metres.

Available warehouse support includes:

- Receiving orders from multiple suppliers
- Recording package quantities and visible condition
- Cargo consolidation for one international shipment
- Shipping-mark and label application
- Sorting goods by order or destination
- Palletising and export packing coordination
- Loading preparation for FCL and LCL cargo

This operating model is useful for importers purchasing furniture, building materials, equipment, e-commerce stock, samples or smaller orders from several Chinese suppliers.

## Principal trade lanes

Our strongest ocean-freight lanes include Australia and New Zealand, South America and Africa. We also coordinate customs-clearance and delivery solutions in Australia, North America and Southeast Asia through the appropriate destination-side service arrangements.

Service scope and responsibility are confirmed shipment by shipment. Import rules, duties, inspections and destination charges depend on the cargo and importing country.

## How we work with importers

An importer can send one set of cargo details to a dedicated China-side contact. We then review the shipment requirements and identify missing information before a booking is confirmed.

For an initial assessment, please provide:

- Product name, material and intended use
- Supplier pickup city and address
- Package count and packaging type
- Packed dimensions and gross weight
- Cargo-ready date
- Destination port or delivery postcode
- Required service scope and Incoterm
- Details of batteries, liquids, powders, chemicals or wooden packaging

## Our operating principle

TengYoda's operating principle is simple: serve global importers by solving practical logistics requirements from the China side. That means communicating clearly, identifying foreseeable risks early and taking responsibility for the work within our confirmed service scope.
