What does a China freight forwarder do for an importer?

A China freight forwarder connects the supplier, origin transport, warehouse, export process and international carrier. The practical goal is to make sure the cargo that was purchased can leave China with suitable packing, consistent documents and a shipping plan that matches the destination requirements.

TengYoda coordinates the China side of international shipments for overseas importers. Our main services include factory pickup, supplier-order consolidation, FCL and LCL ocean freight, air freight, export customs coordination and shipping-document support.

Information needed before we can quote

A reliable quotation starts with the packed cargo, not only the product description. Please provide:

  1. Product name, material and intended use
  2. Number and type of packages
  3. External dimensions and gross weight of every package
  4. Supplier pickup address or city
  5. Destination port or final delivery postcode
  6. Cargo-ready date
  7. Incoterm agreed with the supplier
  8. Photographs of the product and proposed packaging
  9. Details of batteries, liquids, powders, magnets, chemicals or timber

If the dimensions are still estimated, the quotation should be treated as provisional until the final packing details are available.

Services coordinated from China

Factory pickup and domestic transport

We can review the supplier location, loading conditions and required vehicle. Oversized pieces, machinery and fragile products require individual dimensions, weights and lifting information before collection.

Foshan warehouse consolidation

Goods from several suppliers can be received at our Lishui or Lecong warehouse, recorded and prepared for one international shipment. Each warehouse covers more than 3,000 square metres and can support labelling, sorting and palletising.

FCL and LCL ocean freight

FCL is generally considered when the cargo can use a full container or requires fewer handling points. LCL can be practical for trial orders and smaller volumes, but destination handling charges must be included in the comparison.

Air freight and international express

Time-sensitive orders may be assessed for air freight or express. Cargo restrictions, chargeable weight and destination requirements need to be checked before acceptance.

How to evaluate a freight quotation

Do not compare only the main freight number. Ask each provider to identify:

  • Origin pickup and warehouse charges
  • Export declaration and documentation
  • Ocean or air freight and carrier surcharges
  • Destination terminal and shipping-line charges
  • Customs clearance, duty and tax responsibility
  • Delivery, unloading and equipment requirements
  • Inspection, storage, demurrage and detention exclusions

The least expensive freight line is not necessarily the lowest landed logistics cost.

Frequently asked questions

Can you collect goods from several Chinese suppliers?

Yes. We can plan pickup or warehouse receiving from several suppliers, provided every order uses the assigned shipping mark and warehouse reference.

Can you provide door delivery?

Door-delivery availability depends on the destination, cargo and importer requirements. The quotation must state whether duty, tax, inspections and exceptional charges are included or excluded.

Can you guarantee the sailing date?

We can book against the carrier's current schedule, but port congestion, weather, equipment availability and carrier changes can affect actual departure and arrival dates.
