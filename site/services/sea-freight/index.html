Bring every step together.

PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Clear answers before your cargo moves.

Which trade lanes do you focus on for sea freight from China?

Our focus trade lanes are Oceania, Africa and South America. Share the destination port, cargo details and ready date for an FCL or LCL assessment. Specific sailings, carrier acceptance and destination handling are confirmed for each booking; a regional focus does not imply every port has a direct sailing.

What information is needed for an FCL container shipping quote from China?

Provide the packed dimensions, gross weight, cargo-ready date, pickup location and destination port or delivery address. State the preferred container size if known. Compare ocean freight with origin and destination charges, not just the container rate.
