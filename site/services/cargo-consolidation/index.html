PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Clear answers before your cargo moves.

How can I combine shipments from multiple suppliers in China?

Prepare a supplier list with order references, expected arrival dates and package details. Agree the receiving instructions and checking scope first. Once the required goods arrive, compare the combined shipment with an air, LCL or FCL plan based on final measurements.

Should I use a Yiwu or Shenzhen consolidation warehouse?

Choose the receiving location around supplier addresses, cargo-ready dates and the export route, rather than the city name alone. TengYoda can review Qingdao, Yiwu and Shenzhen receiving options. Confirm the location, appointment and shipping marks with Vinson before delivery.
