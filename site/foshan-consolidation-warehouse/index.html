Combine several supplier orders into one export shipment

Importers often purchase from factories in Foshan, Guangzhou and other Chinese cities. Shipping every order separately can create duplicated handling and incomplete visibility. A consolidation warehouse provides one receiving point before the international shipment is booked or loaded.

TengYoda operates two logistics warehouses in Lishui and Lecong, Foshan. Each facility covers more than 3,000 square metres and supports cargo consolidation, labelling, sorting, palletising and shipment preparation.

What happens when cargo arrives?

The agreed receiving procedure can include:

  1. Match the delivery to the importer and supplier reference.
  2. Record the number and type of packages received.
  3. Take arrival photographs when included in the service scope.
  4. Report visible carton or crate damage.
  5. Measure the final external dimensions and gross weight.
  6. Apply shipping marks or destination labels.
  7. Sort packages by purchase order or destination.
  8. Prepare the cargo for palletising, LCL handoff or container loading.

This is not automatically a product-quality inspection. Product counting, functional inspection or detailed quality control must be agreed separately before delivery.

Suitable cargo and importer profiles

Foshan consolidation is commonly useful for:

  • Furniture and lighting purchased from several suppliers
  • Building materials and home-improvement products
  • E-commerce stock from multiple factories
  • Machinery parts and small equipment
  • Trial orders and commercial samples
  • Buyers who need one export shipment and one set of shipping instructions

Very heavy, oversized, dangerous or temperature-controlled cargo requires prior approval and specific handling arrangements.

Supplier instructions before delivery

Every supplier should receive:

  • Correct warehouse address and contact details
  • Importer name or account reference
  • Purchase-order number
  • Required shipping mark
  • Delivery appointment requirement
  • Package and pallet instructions
  • Cargo-ready and expected-delivery date

Suppliers should not send unidentified cargo. Missing references can delay receiving and make it difficult to match the goods to the correct shipment.

Packing and palletising review

Export packaging should protect the goods during warehouse handling, loading, vessel movement and destination delivery. Cartons should be strong enough for their weight and the expected stacking method.

If timber is used, destination biosecurity requirements must be reviewed. Plywood, compliant treated timber or non-wood alternatives may be more suitable, depending on the cargo and importing country.

Consolidation into FCL or LCL

Once the final packages have arrived, the actual volume and gross weight can be checked against the transport plan.

FCL may be considered when the combined orders justify a full container or when handling needs to be reduced. LCL may be appropriate for a smaller combined volume. The decision should consider package size, cargo value, handling risk, origin costs and destination charges.

Frequently asked questions

Can you collect directly from each supplier?

Yes, subject to the supplier locations, cargo size, loading conditions and agreed pickup scope. Suppliers can also deliver to the nominated warehouse.

Can the warehouse check product quality?

Basic receiving confirms packages and visible condition. Detailed product inspection, model verification or testing needs a separately agreed checklist and service.

Can furniture from Lecong be consolidated with other products?

Yes, after reviewing package dimensions, protection, stackability and loading compatibility with the other cargo.
